**Circuit ends the guesswork.** We are a provenance protocol that ensures no garment is made until it is confirmed and recorded on-chain."

As resales happen in the feed, you’ll see our **Projected Royalties** stat increase. We are turning one-time sales into a perpetual revenue stream for African designers."
