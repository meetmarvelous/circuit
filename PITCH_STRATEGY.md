# Circuit Hackathon Pitch Strategy 🏆

This guide outlines how to present the Circuit MVP to judges and investors for maximum impact.

## 1. The Hook (The "Problem")
Start by mentioning that **fashion is the 2nd most polluting industry**, largely because brands produce thousands of units they *hope* will sell. When they don't, they are burned or landfilled.
- **The Statistic**: $500B worth of unsold stock every year.
- **The Solution**: Circuit — A protocol where demand is proven before the first needle is threaded.

## 2. The Demo Walkthrough (3 Minutes)
When demoing the dashboard, follow this flow:

### Phase A: The "Primary Drop" (0-40 Units)
- **Point out the "Drop Zero Progress"**: Explain that every unit shown is already paid for instantly via **Helio**. Highlight that the checkout automatically mints the NFT to the buyer's wallet, demonstrating real Web3 automation without custom backend code.
- **Show the "Verification Maze"**: Explain that as the physical team in Lagos completes a garment, they "link" it. The judges will see the **"Minting"** status flip to **"Verified"** in real-time.
- **The "Inspect" Moment**: Click "Inspect" on a verified item. Show the **Digital Product Passport**. This is the garment's birth certificate on Solana.

### Phase B: The "Magic" of Web3 (Royalties)
- Wait for the dashboard to hit **40/40**.
- **The Pivot**: "Now that we are sold out, how does the designer keep making money?"
- **Show a "Resale" entry**: Point to the "Projected Royalties" stat increasing. Explain that because the garment is linked to an NFT, **Circuit captures 7% of every future resale** automatically. This is impossible in Web2 fashion.

## 3. Key Talking Points (Answers for Judges)
- **"Why Solana?"**: Low fees ($0.00025 per mint) and high speed are essential for consumer-scale fashion. SMS (Solana Mobile Stack) is the future of our NFC interactions.
- **"How does the NFC work?"**: The chip is embedded in the label. Scanning it with a phone pulls up the Digital Product Passport directly from the blockchain — proving it's not a counterfeit.
- **"What is the business model?"**: We take a platform fee from designers and a share of the perpetual royalties. We are the **infrastructure** for the next generation of high-end fashion.

## 4. The Closing Statement
"Circuit is more than a dashboard. It's a movement to make luxury fashion sustainable, verifiable, and perpetually valuable. We are starting in Lagos, but the protocol is global."
