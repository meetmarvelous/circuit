Helio — Taking Pre-Order Payments & Minting Automatically (No Code)

Instead of collecting fiat on Paystack and manually minting NFTs later, you will use **Helio.pay**. Helio is a Web3 payment platform built on Solana that lets you accept USDC/SOL and automatically airdrop an NFT upon payment.

1. Go to hel.io and connect your Phantom wallet.
2. Create a new "Pay Link" for your drop. Set the price (e.g., $45 USDC, roughly equivalent to ₦65,000).
3. Under the "After Payment" or "NFT Automation" settings in Helio, you can configure the checkout to automatically mint and transfer a Digital Product Passport (NFT) to the buyer's wallet the second their payment clears.
4. Set the NFT royalty percentage to 7%.

This creates a seamless, instant Web3 transaction. The buyer pays, the funds enter your wallet instantly, and they receive their permanent Solana record immediately. No manual minting required, saving you hours of work per drop.

NFC Tags — Connecting the Physical Garment to the Record

Source the tags from AliExpress or Computer Village in Lagos. Ask specifically for NTAG213 sticker tags.

To program each tag, download the NFC Tools app on your phone. Open the app, tap Write, then Add a Record, then URL. 
Paste in the URL of the garment's Solana record (the NFT that Helio automatically minted to the buyer). You can find this on solscan.io. Write the URL to the tag.

Now anyone who taps that tag with a smartphone will be taken directly to the garment's permanent Solana record.

For the physical installation: sew a small fabric pocket into the inside lining of the garment, near the hem or the back neck seam. The tag sits inside that pocket.

The sequence for each garment, in order:

One — Buyer pays via your Helio link. Funds settle instantly in USDC.
Two — Helio automatically mints and sends the Digital Product Passport (NFT) to their wallet.
Three — Garment enters production. You sew it.
Four — You find the minted NFT on Solscan and programme its URL onto an NFC tag.
Five — You sew the NFC tag into the garment lining.
Six — Garment ships. Buyer receives it, taps the tag, and verifies their ownership on-chain.

That is the complete Web3-native MVP. No developer required until you are ready to build a custom smart contract, but it already uses 100% Solana rails.
